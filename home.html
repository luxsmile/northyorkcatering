Can I ask for any type of food? +

Yes. Tell us what you have in mind and we will deliver it.

Can I place a last-minute order? +

Please order at least 24 hours ahead. For anything sooner, call us and we will tell you what is possible.

Is there a minimum order? +

Delivery orders have a $200 minimum. There is no minimum for pickup.

How does delivery work? +

You can choose delivery or pickup. Delivery is 10% of your order, up to a maximum of $50. Pickup has no delivery fee.

What is the cancellation policy? +

Cancel 4 or more days before your order date at no charge. Cancellations within 4 days are charged 30% of the order.
